# Cognitive Holobiont Research

Long-horizon, evidence-driven research program investigating the Cognitive Holobiont architecture described in `The_Cognitive_Holobiont_Treatise.pdf`.

## Research principle

The treatise is treated as a hypothesis/specification, not as established scientific fact. Every major claim will be tested against primary literature, supporting evidence, contradictory evidence, replications, limitations, and competing architectures.

## Objectives

- Build an exhaustive literature map across AI, ML, neuroscience, biology, distributed systems, robotics, security, cognitive science, and recursive self-improvement.
- Trace citation chains from seminal work through follow-ups, competing approaches, replications, critiques, negative results, and recent developments.
- Maintain a claim-to-evidence matrix for every major Holobiont hypothesis.
- Record contradictions and unresolved questions explicitly.
- Analyze mathematical assumptions and proposed equations rather than accepting them at face value.
- Establish falsification criteria before implementation.
- Identify what is already known, what is plausible, what is unsupported, and what may be genuinely novel.
- Produce a revised architecture only after the evidence warrants it.

9. Uncertainty, OOD detection, and AI immune systems
10. Adversarial ML and Byzantine fault tolerance
11. Distributed memory and identity
12. Neuroscience and biological analogies
13. Embodied and decentralized intelligence
14. Consciousness and cognitive architectures
15. Recursive self-improvement and self-rearchitecture
16. Distributed-systems theory and common-mode failure
17. Compute, communication, and scaling economics

## Repository structure

- `research/` — master research documents and evolving synthesis
- `literature/` — literature organized by research area
- `papers/` — structured paper notes and bibliographic records
- `mathematics/` — formal models, assumptions, and derivations
- `experiments/` — hypotheses, baselines, metrics, and falsification designs
- `architecture/` — architecture versions and design decisions
- `sources/` — source indexes and provenance information
