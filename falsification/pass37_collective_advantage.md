# Falsification Framework — Collective Advantage
**Pass 37 · 2026-11-22**

The distinctive claim is that a federation of independent, heterogeneous specialist organs creates capabilities, reliability or continuity that simpler architectures cannot reproduce at matched resources.

## Primary falsifiers

1. **No positive frontier shift:** Across representative task families, the federation is never better than the best single-model, best-of-K, MoE/router or conventional MAS baseline at comparable total resources.
2. **No causal transfer:** In private-evidence tasks, removing or swapping the evidence-owning organ does not change the result, or a single model with the same union of evidence reproduces the gain.
3. **No interaction advantage:** Positive coalition interactions disappear after matching compute, token count, information access, selection and verifier budget.
4. **No durable specialization:** Specialist capabilities collapse under shared training, role changes or task distribution shift, and cannot be retained better than ordinary adapters or model routing.
5. **No resilience advantage:** Component failures cause the same or greater degradation than a simpler replicated service or checkpoint/backup system.
6. **Synergy metric has no predictive validity:** PID or other collective-structure measures fail to predict independently scored utility under held-out interventions.
7. **Routing fails to abstain:** The selector activates multiple organs on compact/sequential tasks even when a single model has higher expected utility at lower cost.
8. **Advantage is not reproducible:** Results depend on one benchmark, one model family, one evaluator, one seed or a non-independent judge.

## Required positive evidence

- A pre-registered resource-adjusted effect on held-out task instances.
- Confidence intervals and repeated runs across model families.
- Causal ownership swaps and ablations for private evidence.
- A strong single-model multi-output and best-of-K baseline.
- An external evaluator or executable ground truth.
- Independent measurement of latency, cost, compute, context, bandwidth, tool use and verification.
- A failure condition where the federation retains a measurable capability or service level that a simpler baseline cannot.

## Separate outcomes

Report separately:
- task utility;
- unique information recovered;
- parallel speedup;
- reliability and error amplification;
- resilience under component failure;
- memory/continuity;
- safety and privacy;
- resource efficiency.

A gain in one category must not be used to claim gains in all the others.

## Stop / narrow criteria

If no distinctive advantage appears, narrow the project to the domains where the evidence supports it: selective heterogeneous routing, private-evidence integration, parallel task decomposition, independent verification or fault-tolerant service composition. A smaller justified architecture is preferable to an unsupported claim of universal collective intelligence.
