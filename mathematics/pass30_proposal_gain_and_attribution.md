# Pass 30 Mathematics

Net gain = verified utility of collaboration minus verified utility of a practical selector using the same initial proposals and verifier budget.

Best-initial regression = best initial proposal score minus collaborative final score. This is an evaluator-side ceiling, not a deployable baseline.

Topology gain = utility after learned pruning minus utility after random pruning at the same structural budget.

Fixed-continuation removal and rerun-with-replanning estimate different contribution effects. Trace attribution must be compared with both.
