## New evaluation rule

A latent-MAS or Holobiont result should not be credited as distributed intelligence unless (1) the task requires sender-private information, (2) sender-message interventions demonstrate causal dependence, (3) receiver-self and other-example controls are included, and (4) centralized/self-conditioning/MoE/text-MAS baselines are matched on compute, memory, and externally available information.
