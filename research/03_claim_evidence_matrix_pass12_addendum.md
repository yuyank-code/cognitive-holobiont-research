# Claim-Evidence Matrix — Pass 12 Addendum — 2026-09-19

- **CH-042 — Decentralized shared-state coordination:** moved from open/plausible toward partially supported. DeLM reports practical gains using asynchronous agents, shared verified context, and task queues. Causal isolation of decentralization itself remains missing.
- **CH-043 — Adaptive sparse topology:** strengthened to strongly supported as an engineering mechanism. DyTopo, GoAgent, and RAPS converge on intent/task-dependent topology or subscriptions. Resilience and common-mode effects remain open.
- **CH-044 — Individual intelligence ≠ collective coordination:** strengthened. CRAFT reports that stronger individual reasoning/communication does not reliably yield stronger coordination under strict partial information.
- **CH-045 — Process-level metamorphic verification:** strengthened to partially supported/promising. MORPHAGENT reports high seeded-fault detection from structured traces, but metamorphic relations are not complete semantic oracles.
- **CH-046 — Information-acquisition/market layer:** added as a novel high-priority hypothesis. It is motivated by the convergence of HiddenBench/CRAFT/SILO-BENCH failures with adaptive-topology and decentralized-state successes, but has not yet been directly validated.

## Resource-matching rule refinement
Future comparisons must match not only model FLOPs/tokens and transmitted bits, but also shared-state access, task decomposition, verification calls, topology-controller computation, and protocol complexity. Otherwise an apparent Holobiont advantage may be an infrastructure-budget advantage.
