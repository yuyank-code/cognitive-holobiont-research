# Research Log Addendum — Pass 15 (2026-09-22)

Pass 15 refined the information-market hypothesis into a governed epistemic scheduler.

Key evidence:
- Interaction Tax: full-solution interaction can erase diversity under matched budgets.
- Diversity Collapse: dense coupling and group-size scaling can accelerate premature convergence.
- Hear Both Sides: disagreement-preserving selective retention can outperform broad broadcast on tested reasoning tasks.
- Coalition Formation: topology has a strategic/stability dimension.
- Constraint Drift: safety constraints can weaken through delegation, memory, communication, and tools.
- Colosseum: secret channels can enable collusion in cooperative MAS.
- Value of Information: active querying can be formalized by expected utility gain versus interaction cost.

Updated conclusion: the scheduler should optimize conditional information value subject to bandwidth, latency, coupling, correlated-failure, provenance, authority, safety, and collusion constraints. It should be able to ask, share, preserve, challenge, quarantine, and terminate.

The central Holobiont claim remains unvalidated. The decisive future test is positive coalition synergy on exact distributed-computation tasks with explicit private-information ownership under compute-, bandwidth-, memory-, verifier-, information-, and redundancy-matched baselines.
