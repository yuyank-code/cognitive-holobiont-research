# Claim–Evidence Matrix Addendum — Pass 27 (2026-10-16)

| Claim | Evidence added | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Vote share/consensus alone estimates panel reliability | CAGE-Cal (arXiv:2605.30653) reports topology-dependent false consensus and improves ECE/AUROC using matched no-communication counterfactual graphs | Naive claim contradicted; dependency-aware calibration promising in tested benchmarks |
| Information-theoretic synergy establishes superior task performance | ICLR 2026 Emergent Coordination measures synergy in one group guessing task; the performance relationship is conditional and mediation is marginal | Structural synergy measurable; task-general performance claim unvalidated |
| Multi-agent systems cannot outperform single agents | AgentWorld reports 52.0% success for its strongest primary MAS versus 28.6% single-agent baseline on deliberately collaborative tasks | Blanket claim contradicted in that sandbox; broad generalization open |
| A MAS win proves Cognitive Holobiont-specific synergy | AgentWorld does not test all resource-matched alternatives or the full Treatise architecture | Unvalidated |
| More communication monotonically improves quality | CAGE-Cal observes communication-induced individual regressions; AgentWorld and MASBench support selective routing/protocols | Contradicted as a general rule |
| Fault tolerance is automatic with more agents | MAS-FIRE reports architecture- and fault-class-dependent failure and recovery | Unsupported; fault injection and recovery metrics required |
| Lower message-token count means lower total cost | MARS/MASBench count different resource subsets; MASBench excludes local reasoning tokens | Not established without total-cost accounting |
| Hypernetwork regeneration, machine consciousness, or immortality follows from these results | No direct evidence in this pass | Unvalidated |

Keep structural dependence/synergy, verified task utility, causal contribution of messages/actions, confidence calibration, and fault resilience as separate constructs.
