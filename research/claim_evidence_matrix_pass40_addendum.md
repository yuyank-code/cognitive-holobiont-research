# Claim–Evidence Matrix Addendum — Pass 40
**Date:** 2026-11-25  
**Topic:** Capability recovery, Hypernetwork DNA and specialist regeneration.

| Treatise claim / hypothesis | New evidence | Status | Critical limitation / decisive test |
|---|---|---|---|
| A hypernetwork can repair a specialist after capability loss. | HyperRepair generates a gated repair side-net for a surviving fine-tuned model from activation fingerprints; two backbone families and twelve LoRA adaptation datasets. | **Conditionally supported for post-hoc restoration** | Target model and shared pretrained-backbone lineage remain. Test after removing the target checkpoint/base separately. |
| Surviving organs can regenerate a missing organ. | No new source in this pass demonstrates peer-assisted recovery after target weights, adapters, unique data and teacher access are removed. | **Unproven distinctive claim** | Capability-deletion benchmark with explicit surviving-information matrix and offline evaluator vault. |
| Failure experience can become durable learned capability. | Self-Direction turns detected failures into small LoRA adapters; improves tested GAIA, Claw-Eval and Terminal-Bench results; some related-task transfer. | **Supported for procedural adaptation in bounded workflows** | Requires failure signal, feedback, adapter training and routing; model-specific; misrouting can hurt. Compare with Skills.md and retrieval. |
| Forgotten capabilities can be restored without retraining. | DG-Hard uses both base and fine-tuned checkpoints and spectral filtering of their weight delta. | **Supported as a checkpoint-pair repair hypothesis** | Requires both checkpoints; relies on low-rank signal/noise-residual assumption; diffuse useful signal can be lost. |
| Multiple specialists can be combined into a new organ. | PRISM screens and repairs destructive merges of available same-base specialists. | **Conditionally supported for model integration** | All source specialist checkpoints remain available; merge is not regeneration and can lose specialization. |
| A deleted specialist's behavior can be copied from another organ. | Distillation study reports high private-value recovery from teacher-generated trajectories in same-family Qwen3 GSM8K setup. | **Conditional on continuing teacher access and sufficient trajectories** | Not recovery after information deletion; cross-family/cross-scale capability transfer remains open. |
| Repeated self-modification preserves future learning ability. | ICML 2026 continual-learning study identifies spectral collapse associated with loss of plasticity and tests rank-preserving regularization. | **Risk supported; Holobiont application unresolved** | Test long-horizon specialist adaptation and future-task plasticity, not only current task score. |
| Exact original weights can be reconstructed from peers. | No evidence establishes this without sufficient identifying information. | **Unsupported as a general claim** | Keep exact parameter reconstruction separate from behavioral equivalence. |

## Required evidence labels

- **Treatise claim:** original design ambition.
- **External result:** a paper's reported result in its stated setup.
- **Inference:** a design consequence drawn from those results.
- **Open hypothesis:** not yet established by a decisive Holobiont experiment.

## Pass 40 status update

The key change is taxonomic: rollback, local repair, post-hoc forgetting repair, procedural adaptation, specialist merging, behavioral distillation and peer-assisted regeneration are separate recovery classes. Only the last directly tests the distinctive Hypernetwork DNA ambition.
