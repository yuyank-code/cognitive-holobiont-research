# Contradictions and Open Problems — Pass 24 (2026-10-13)

## Contradiction log

1. **Information recovery vs. error spread:** more communication can expose a decisive private fact but can also spread an erroneous claim. Routing must account for both insight propagation and error propagation.
2. **Memory continuity vs. temporal validity:** persistent state reduces rediscovery, but old state may become inadmissible even when retrieved accurately.
3. **Consolidation quality vs. source quality:** perfect consolidation does not remove all source-induced bias; conversely, some bias effects disappear under tested models/conditions.
4. **Lineage vs. authority:** provenance is useful but can be laundered unless origin/authority is bound before transformation.
5. **Protocol efficiency vs. truth:** typed communication can shorten messages and improve coordination, but a protocol that produces agreement does not by itself establish correctness.
6. **Sparse vs. dense topology:** sparse networks contain errors but can suppress useful minority facts; dense networks disseminate insight but also errors.
7. **Benchmark score vs. total resources:** communication-token gains are not full system-cost gains when private reasoning, prompts, tools, latency, and verification are excluded.
8. **Causal attribution vs. trace description:** a graph of actions/messages is not a causal estimate unless interventions validate its edges.

## Open problems

- Estimate information value under privacy/authorization constraints.
- Preserve write-time origin and authority across summaries, tools, delegation, and compression.
- Propagate invalidation to dependent plans/actions without discarding independent evidence.
- Protect valid minority facts from majority- or confidence-based rejection.
- Learn topology switching rules that generalize across reasoning, scheduling, coding, and embodied settings.
- Define fair resource matching when internal reasoning tokens are hidden or non-comparable.
- Measure causal action contribution under interacting messages and correlated failures.
- Test whether Holobiont-specific synergy remains after strong single-agent, ensemble, MoE, and shared-context controls.
