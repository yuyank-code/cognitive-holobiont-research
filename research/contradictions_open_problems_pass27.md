# Contradictions and Open Problems — Pass 27 (2026-10-16)

## New contradictions
1. **Consensus vs calibrated reliability:** identical vote shares can reflect independent corroboration or communication-induced correlated error. Confidence must account for how the panel reached agreement.
2. **Structural synergy vs useful capability:** partial-information decomposition can detect non-additive temporal structure, but the relationship to verified task performance remains task-dependent and potentially endogenous.
3. **Trace graph vs causal necessity:** model-inferred action/message attribution is diagnostic, but necessity requires interventions such as removal, delay, or corruption of the purportedly enabling action.
4. **Counterfactual insight vs compute budget:** matched no-communication panels help estimate interaction-induced dependence, but extra independent runs and candidate-topology runs must be charged.
5. **Closed-loop recovery vs corrupted foundations:** iterative critics can repair selected errors, while blind-trust instructions, contradictory premises, or compromised reviewers can still cause systemic failure.
6. **Local correction vs global recovery:** detecting or fixing one fault does not guarantee end-to-end task success; measure detection, correction, containment, and final recovery separately.
7. **Communication efficiency vs total efficiency:** message-token savings can coexist with greater private reasoning, verifier, retry, and latency costs.

## Open problems
- Validate CAGE-style confidence under adversarial agents, shared tools/retrieval, and temporal state changes.
- Estimate task-conditional marginal coupling value before spending the full interaction budget.
- Establish a task-general intervention-based definition of collective synergy.
- Compare inferred action-graph contribution with measured outcome changes after action/message ablation.
- Test whether adaptive closed-loop recovery improves success without excessive false alarms, privacy leakage, or verifier costs.
- Require held-out domains, ownership permutations, and total-resource accounting before making a distinctive Holobiont claim.
