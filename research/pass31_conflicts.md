# Pass 31 Evidence Conflicts

1. MemGuard reports gains from lifecycle governance, while Supersede reports that temporal fact currency remains difficult. These findings address different targets and should be tested factorially.
2. Persistent verifier metadata can reduce repeated misuse, but verifier labels are imperfect; false-positive admission can make an error durable.
3. Provenance helps trace a claim to a source, but does not prove that the source is current or true.
4. Memory compression can improve efficiency, but compression metrics do not establish correct temporal updates.
5. Adaptive communication may help on hidden-profile tasks, but its local surrogate guarantee is not a guarantee of end-task correctness; SILO-BENCH shows that communication volume alone is insufficient.

Decisive test: compare retrieval, verifier-only filtering, lifecycle governance, temporal versioning, and adaptive routing under matched budgets and independent outcome checks. Report stale actions, benign false rejection, authorization outcomes, and total cost separately.
