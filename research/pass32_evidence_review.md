# Pass 32 Evidence Review
Date: 2026-10-22

The reviewed preprints address complementary questions: PACMI (arXiv:2610.05732) studies validity updates across dependent records; TOKI (arXiv:2606.06240) studies consistency of versioned writes; arXiv:2606.24322 studies preservation of source identity across transformations; arXiv:2606.04990 surveys execution and evidence tracing; STALE (arXiv:2605.06527) tests whether later information changes earlier conclusions. These are different properties, and the combined design has not been evaluated end to end.
