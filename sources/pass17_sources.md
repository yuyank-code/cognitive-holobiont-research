# Pass 17 — Source Index (2026-09-24)

All sources were freely accessible during this research pass. They are treated as evidence, not as validation of the originating Holobiont specification.

1. Zhang et al. (2026), **SILO-BENCH: A Scalable Environment for Evaluating Distributed Coordination in Multi-Agent LLM Systems**, ACL 2026. https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1354/ — primary negative evidence for communication-to-computation failure.
2. Yang et al. (2026), **When 20 Agents Fail to Sort: The Distributed Sorting Benchmark for Scalable Multi-Agent Systems**, Findings of ACL 2026. https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1698/ — distributed sorting failures plus CAMOC positive protocol intervention.
3. Babu et al. (2026), **Consilience: Conformally Calibrated Communication Control for Hidden-Profile Multi-Agent Reasoning**, arXiv:2608.20564 — adaptive, calibrated communication-control evidence.
4. Huang et al. (2026), **MANTA: Multi-Agent Network Topology Adaptation for Self-Evolving Multi-Agent Systems**, arXiv:2607.28527 — inference-time topology adaptation.
5. Lu et al. (2026), **DyTopo: Dynamic Topology Routing for Multi-Agent Reasoning via Semantic Matching**, arXiv:2602.06039 — round-conditioned sparse routing.
6. Sun et al. (2026), **TopoDIM: One-shot Topology Generation of Diverse Interaction Modes for Multi-Agent Systems**, Findings of ACL 2026. https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.207/ — topology generation and communication efficiency.
7. Duan et al. (2026), **Heterogeneous Information-Bottleneck Coordination Graphs for Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning**, arXiv:2605.17393 — mathematical graph/bandwidth allocation framework.
8. Mao & Mirhoseini (2026), **Decentralized Multi-Agent Systems with Shared Context**, arXiv:2606.10662 — verified shared-context decentralization as a competing architecture.
9. Riedl (2026), **Emergent Coordination in Multi-Agent Language Models** — information-theoretic emergence/synergy measurement direction.
10. Chen et al. (2026), **Diversity Collapse in Multi-Agent LLM Systems**, Findings ACL 2026. https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.13/ — negative evidence on dense coupling and independence loss.
11. El Yagoubi et al. (2026), **AgentLeak: A Full-Stack Benchmark for Privacy Leakage in Multi-Agent LLM Systems**, arXiv:2602.11510 — internal-channel governance/security evidence.
12. Peng et al. (2026), **StateBridge: Training-free Hidden-state Alignment for Latent Communication in LLM Multi-Agent Systems**, arXiv:2608.13317 — positive latent communication evidence, but not proof of distributed cognition.
13. Brito & Baquero (2026), **When Latent Agents Lie: KV-Cache Integrity in Multi-Agent LLM Collaboration**, arXiv:2606.28958 — latent-channel integrity/security evidence.
