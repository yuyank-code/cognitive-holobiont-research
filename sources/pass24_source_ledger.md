## Pass 25 — joint topology, privacy, and interactions (2026-10-14)

14. MASBench — arXiv:2610.04672, submitted 2026-10-03, preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04672. Protocol/memory/routing under partial observability; communication metric excludes local reasoning tokens.
15. Topology Matters: Measuring Memory Leakage in Multi-Agent LLMs (MAMA) — Findings of ACL 2026, peer-reviewed. https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1980/. Synthetic PII extraction across six topologies and 4–6 agents; denser connectivity, shorter attacker-target distance, and higher target centrality increase leakage in this threat model.
16. Before It Persists: Write-Time Defense for Multimodal Agent Memory (SAGE-Mem) — ICML 2026 SCALE Workshop, public implementation/artifacts. https://agampandeyy.github.io/sage-mem/ ; https://github.com/AGAMPANDEYY/sage-mem. Write-time admission reduces attack rates in tested suites; reported BCU exposes security-utility trade-off.
17. E2-Explainer — arXiv:2608.12921 v2, preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12921. Edge masking and topology pruning; single-edge interventions can miss multi-edge interactions.
18. GoAgent — arXiv:2603.19677, preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19677. Group-level topology and conditional information bottleneck; benchmark-specific baseline.
19. HiddenBench — ICML 2026, PMLR 306:69250–69268, peer-reviewed. https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/li26ej.html. Distributed information is often not elicited/integrated; full-profile comparison is diagnostic, not deployment-matched.
