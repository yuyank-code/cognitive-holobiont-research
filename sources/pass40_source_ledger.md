# Source Ledger — Pass 40
**Date:** 2026-11-25  
**Topic:** Capability repair, hypernetwork recovery, distillation and regeneration limits.

## Preprints

1. Tang et al. (2026), **HyperRepair: Recovering Forgotten General Capabilities of Fine-Tuned Language Models**, arXiv:2610.41147, submitted 30 Oct 2026.  
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41147  
   Hypernetwork-generated, gated repair side-net for surviving fine-tuned models; two backbone families, twelve LoRA adaptation datasets, fifteen general benchmarks. Preprint. Not peer-reviewed in the source record.

2. Kansal et al. (2026), **Don't Forget What You Just Learned: Self-Directed Repair Turns Agent Mistakes Into Lasting Lessons**, arXiv:2610.31846, submitted 22 Oct 2026.  
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31846  
   Failure-derived LoRA adapters and persistent lesson routing; GAIA, Claw-Eval, Terminal-Bench 2.1. Preprint. Model-specific adapters, failure-signal dependence and routing risk.

3. Abro & Tahir (2026), **Spectral Unforgetting: Post-Hoc Recovery of Damaged Capabilities Without Retraining**, arXiv:2605.20296, submitted 19 May 2026.  
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20296  
   DG-Hard spectral filtering of fine-tuning deltas; requires both pretrained base and fine-tuned descendant. Preprint. Assumes useful update signal is spectrally concentrated; diffuse useful signal can be removed.

4. Lee et al. (2026), **Predicting and Repairing Merge Collapse in Large Language Models**, arXiv:2610.03199, submitted 2 Oct 2026.  
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03199  
   PRISM predicts and repairs destructive merging of available shared-base specialists. Preprint with public implementation: https://github.com/js-lee-AI/PRISM. Model merging is not missing-organ regeneration.

5. Nie et al. (2026), **Identified-Set Geometry of Distributional Model Extraction under Top-K Censored API Access**, arXiv:2605.10407, submitted 11 May 2026.  
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10407  
   Distinguishes distribution fidelity from downstream capability transfer. Same-family Qwen3 experiments report 96.1% private-value recovery from generation distillation on GSM8K; cross-scale/cross-family capability recovery remains open. Preprint.

## Peer-reviewed conference papers

6. Prakash et al. (2026), **Spectral Collapse Drives Loss of Plasticity in Deep Continual Learning**, ICML 2026, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research 306, pp. 99578–99625.  
   https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/prakash26b.html  
   Studies loss of plasticity and Hessian spectral collapse in continual supervised and reinforcement learning. Relevant risk for repeated organ adaptation; not an LLM regeneration result.

7. Jayasinghe et al. (2026), **Cerebellar-Inspired Residual Control for Fault Recovery: From Inference-Time Adaptation to Structural Consolidation**, ICML 2026, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research 306, pp. 50986–51010.  
   https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/jayasinghe26a.html  
   Frozen-policy residual correction under robotic faults. Supports a bounded correction-path design pattern; not evidence of language-model specialist regeneration.
